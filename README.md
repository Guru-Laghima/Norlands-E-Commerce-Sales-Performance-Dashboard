# Norlands E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Title
Norlands E-Commerce Store – Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Objectives
- To analyze the overall sales performance of Norlands E-Commerce Store across time, regions, segments, and states.
- To identify top-performing products, customers, and geographic areas.
- To uncover trends in sales volume, order patterns, and average order value.
- To provide clear, actionable insights that support data-driven sales and marketing decisions.

## Dataset Used
Dataset: Norlands E-Commerce Sales Data  
Contains order-level data including sales amount, quantity, customer details, segment, region, state, and order dates.

## Key Questions Explored
1. What is the total sales revenue, quantity sold, and number of orders?
2. How have sales performed over time (2020–2023)?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
4. Which regions and states generate the highest sales?
5. Who are the top customers by sales?
6. What is the average order value and average quantity per order?
7. How is sales performance distributed geographically across the United States?

## Process
1. **Data Cleaning and Preparation**
   - Standardized region, state, and segment names.
   - Handled missing or inconsistent values.
   - Created calculated fields for Average Order Value and Average Quantity per Order.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Aggregated total sales, quantity, and order count.
   - Analyzed year-over-year sales trends.
   - Ranked top states and customers by sales performance.
   - Examined sales contribution by customer segment and region.

## Dashboard Building
- Built an interactive sales performance dashboard using **Google Looker Studio**.
- Included key performance indicators (Total Sales, Total Quantity, Average Order Value, Number of Orders, Average Quantity per Order).
- Created visualizations for Sales Over Time, Sales by Region, Sales by Segment, Top 5 States, Top 5 Customers, geographic heatmap, and a detailed orders table.
- Added filters for Date Range, Region, Segment, and State to allow flexible exploration.

## Recommendations and Insights
1. **Sales Trend**  
   Sales peaked in 2022 (₦609.21K) and declined in 2023.  
   ➔ Investigate the drop in 2023 and develop targeted recovery campaigns.

2. **Segment Performance**  
   Consumer segment accounts for 51.5% of total sales, followed by Corporate (30.6%) and Home Office (17.9%).  
   ➔ Focus marketing efforts on the Consumer segment while exploring growth opportunities in Corporate and Home Office.

3. **Regional Performance**  
   West region leads with the highest sales, followed by East, Central, and South.  
   ➔ Strengthen inventory and promotional activities in the West while improving performance in the South.

4. **Top States**  
   California, New York, Texas, Washington, and Pennsylvania are the top 5 states by sales.  
   ➔ Prioritize these states for exclusive deals, faster shipping options, and localized marketing.

5. **Customer Concentration**  
   A small number of customers (e.g., Sean Miller, Tamara Chand) contribute significantly to revenue.  
   ➔ Develop loyalty programs and personalized offers for top customers to increase retention.

6. **Order Metrics**  
   Average Order Value is ₦465.6 with an average of 7.7 items per order.  
   ➔ Introduce product bundling and upselling strategies to increase both metrics.

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Norlands E-Commerce Store’s sales performance. It enables stakeholders to quickly understand revenue trends, identify high-performing regions and customers, and make informed decisions to drive growth. The interactive filters and clear visualizations turn complex sales data into actionable business insights.
